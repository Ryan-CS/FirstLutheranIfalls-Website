Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

First Lutheran Church (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates a website and Facebook page for sharing church news and events with our community.

What We Do

We use Facebook Login solely to read posts from our own Facebook page and display them on our website. This allows us to share church news, events, and updates with our community.

Data Collection & Storage

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data from visitors or Facebook users. No user information is saved on our servers or shared with third parties.

Facebook Data

Our use of Facebook Login is limited to accessing our own page’s public posts. We do not access any user profiles, friend lists, or other personal information. Our Facebook integration is subject to Facebook’s Data Policy.

Data Deletion

Since we do not store any personal data, there is no user data to delete. If you have any concerns, please contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
