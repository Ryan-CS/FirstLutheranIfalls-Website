Youth

Programs for children, youth, and families to grow in faith together.

Programs

Confirmation Classes

For grades 6th-8th in partnership with our friends at Zion Lutheran Church International Falls. Classes are held on Wednesday nights at 7:00 PM here at First Lutheran during the school year. Students are confirmed fall of 9th grade at their respective churches on Reformation Sunday, the last Sunday in October.

Young people ask good questions. Our role as confirmation leaders is to walk beside these young people and to encourage them to ask these faith questions and learn to journey in the walk of faith and to be community for one another. Together, we will use the Bible, small groups, and each other to find answers to what God has to do with our lives.

High School Youth

During the year we do special activities geared for high school students. Keep an eye out on social media for these events!

First Communion

1st grade (or when families feel the student is ready). Kids are welcome to participate in the Lord's Supper at any age - whether at church or at home. It's helpful to have instructions, though, so we invite first-grade students and their parents to come and learn about communion together. Watch for an opportunity each year, or do some at-home training, if you'd like. If your child is older and hasn't yet received communion or participated in training, no worries. It's never too late to be welcomed to the feast.

Baptism

On the day of baptism, promises are made. Parents and sponsors promise to pray for their child and nurture them in faith, teach them the prayer, creed and commandments, bring them to communion and place in their hands the Bible, and live with them among God's faithful people, so the child will learn to trust God and care for others and the world God made. The congregation, in turn, promise to help the child grow in faith.

Baptism is a communal ritual that welcomes believers to the Christian faith. As Lutherans, we welcome people of all ages, including infants. Prior to a child's baptism, families attend a baptism class to explore the meaning of baptism and to become familiar with the service.
